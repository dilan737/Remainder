F.A.Qs

  1. How do I set up a reminder for my bills?

    Setting up a reminder is easy! Simply log in to your account, go to the “Reminders” section, and click on “Add New Reminder.” Fill in the details of your bill, including the due date and frequency, and save it. You’ll receive notifications as the due date approaches.

  2. How do I edit or delete a reminder?

    To edit or delete a reminder, go to the “Reminders” section, find the reminder you want to change, and click on the “Edit” or “Delete” button. Make the necessary changes and save them.

  3. Is there a fee for using this service?

    Our basic service is free, but we offer premium plans with additional features for a small monthly fee. You can check out our pricing page for more details.

  4. How do I contact customer support?
