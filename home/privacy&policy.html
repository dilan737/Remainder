We take the security of your personal data seriously. All information is stored on secure servers, and we use encryption and other industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

  • Understand how you interact with the platform.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some parts of our services.
